## Merged concept

- **Title mood:** Bright, powerful **horizon** — sun dominates the sky plane; strong late-afternoon warmth.
- **Foreground / near plane:** **Afternoon boat** on calm water, **sunlit hill**, clear **sun** (soft gold, not white-hot), sparkling water (#5B9BD5–#7EC8E3), sky (#87CEEB–#B8D4E8).
- **Distance:** **Zen-balanced blue** mountains and **waterfall** — slate-cerulean / blue-gray (#4B5E6E–#6A7F8C), not royal, indigo, pure sky, or Prussian-heavy; mist; sparse teal-mint **bioluminescent** hints on the cool side.
- **Single coherent** painterly matte painting; soft transition from warm near shore to cool peaks.
